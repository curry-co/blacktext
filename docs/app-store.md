### Promotional text (170)

One page. Plain text. Saved as you type, every version kept, and the same on your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud. Free, open source, and nothing collected.

### Keywords (100)

notepad,scratchpad,writing,draft,journal,minimal,simple,editor,history,versions,sync,find,replace

### Description (4000)

BlackText is one page for typing. No formatting, no fonts, no styles: just plain text, in light or dark.

It saves as you type and keeps a timestamped version of each note as it grows. You never press Save, and you never lose a word. When you're done with a thought, Save+New files it away and gives you a clean page. It's made for the things you jot down for a moment: an idea, a quote, a link, a paragraph you want to rewrite.

• Plain text only. One font, black and white. Paste strips formatting.
• Never lose a word. Saved about a second after you pause, with a version kept every 100 words changed.
• A clean page in one tap. Save+New files the note away, and Undo brings it back.
• Every version kept. Read any earlier version, copy from it, or start a new note from it.
• Find & replace, live as you type, with match case, whole word, regular expressions and preserve case.
• Search across all your notes at once.
• Recently Deleted keeps deleted notes for 30 days.
• Syncs through your own iCloud. Notes, their history and deletions stay the same on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.
• Fast at any size. Ten years of notes open as quickly as the first day's.
• Follows your Text Size setting, and pinching makes the text bigger or smaller.
• With a hardware keyboard, the Mac's shortcuts work too.

YOUR NOTES ARE YOURS
They live on your device. Sync goes through your own private iCloud database: encrypted in transit and on Apple's servers, and end-to-end encrypted if you turn on Advanced Data Protection. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and BlackText collects nothing.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
BlackText is free, with no in-app purchases. Its code is on GitHub under the MIT License.

ON THE MAC
BlackText for Mac is a free download from the website. It has the same page and the same notes, plus a built-in MCP server so AI agents like Claude can read, search and write your notes.

### Notes for the reviewer

BlackText needs no account or sign-in. Type on the page; it saves by itself. Tap Save+New (bottom right, above the keyboard) to file the note away and start a clean page. Open the sidebar with the button at the top left, or by swiping in from the left edge, to see every note; tap the arrow beside a note to see its saved versions. If the device is signed in to iCloud, notes sync through the user's own private iCloud database. The app makes no other network connections and collects no data.
