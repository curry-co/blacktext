Support

Questions, problems and ideas are welcome. Email apps@curry.co, or open an issue on GitHub.

It helps to say which device you're using, its system version, and the BlackText version: on a Mac, it's in BlackText → About BlackText.

To report a security problem, email apps@curry.co rather than opening a public issue.

Common questions

Where are my notes?

On your device, in one file. Every note is listed in the sidebar, most recently edited first. On iPhone, open the sidebar with its button or by swiping in from the left edge.

How do I start a new note?

On a Mac, press ⌘↩ or ⌘N. On iPhone and iPad, tap Save+New. The note you were writing is filed away in the sidebar, and Undo brings it back.

How do I get an earlier version back?

Tap or click the arrow beside a note in the sidebar to see its versions, then choose one to read it. Versions are read-only: copy from one, or start a new note from it.

I deleted a note. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days. On a Mac, choose View → Recently Deleted; on iPhone and iPad, it's at the bottom of the sidebar. Then choose Put Back.

My notes aren't syncing.

  • Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device, with iCloud on.
  • Make sure BlackText is allowed to use iCloud in your device's iCloud settings.
  • Sync needs the App Store app or the Mac app downloaded from this website. Builds you make yourself don't sync.
  • On a Mac, BlackText → iCloud Sync… shows what's waiting to be sent and anything iCloud turned down.

Changes usually arrive within seconds. If two devices change the same note at once, the newer text wins and the other is kept in the note's history.

How do I back up or export my notes?

On a Mac, File → Back Up Notes… writes a complete copy, history included, and File → Export All as Text… writes each note as a .txt file. Time Machine also backs up the notes file.

How do I connect an AI agent?

On a Mac, choose BlackText → Copy MCP Config and paste it into your agent app's MCP settings. For Claude Code, the README has a one-line command.

How do I update the Mac app?

It checks for updates by itself about once a day. To check now, choose BlackText → Check for Updates….

Does BlackText cost anything?

No. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases, and it's open source.
